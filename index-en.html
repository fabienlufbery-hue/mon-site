Discover ASYT

AI that works within your environment.

ASYT is local AI software that combines conversation, document search, memory and automation in a single interface.

Its goal: make your information easier to find, understand and use, without requiring constant reliance on a remote service.

At the core of ASYT

Turn your documents into usable knowledge.

ASYT is designed to go beyond a simple conversation with an AI model.

It combines several technology building blocks to retrieve relevant information, enrich the context of conversations and organize knowledge over time.

Persistent memory
