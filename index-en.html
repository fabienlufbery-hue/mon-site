06 / 08

Your hardware. Your infrastructure. Your AI.

ASYT installs on your computer or your infrastructure, and compatible AI models run directly on that hardware.

ASYT does not build foundation models: it lets you use compatible models designed by their respective publishers.

  • Processor (CPU)

    Can run compatible models; performance depends on the chosen model.

  • Graphics card (GPU)

    A compatible GPU can improve performance.

  • Memory (RAM)

    The model size must suit the available memory.

Not every model runs on every computer: each configuration is assessed case by case.

The initial model download may require an Internet connection. Web features you deliberately trigger require a connection and network exchanges.
