Frequently asked questions

Your questions about ASYT. Answers made clear.

Local operation, documents, memory, automation, compliance: the essentials, without overpromising.

ASYT's local features are designed to work without a connection once the required components are installed. Some operations, such as the initial download of models or retrieving new web pages, may require Internet.

In a fully local configuration, document processing and inference can run on the user's own infrastructure. The exact behavior depends on the configuration and the integrations enabled.

ASYT favors local execution and includes document search, memory and automation features. Performance depends in particular on the model chosen and the hardware available.

Requirements vary depending on the models and features used. A compatible GPU can improve performance, but configurations must be assessed case by case.

ASYT includes a document search system that makes imported content usable. Supported formats and performance depend on the version.

A local persistent memory module is in development and under validation. How it works, in particular saving, retrieving and deleting information, must be checked before any sensitive use.

ASYT is developing scheduling and automation features. Their availability and permissions depend on the version and configuration.

ASYT has a web sources module that can retrieve certain pages and let you consult their content offline later. This does not mean a general-purpose standalone web search engine is available.

ASYT is still in development and under validation. Some features require further testing before deployment in critical environments or environments handling sensitive data.
