About

ASYT was born from a conviction: AI should also be able to run on your own hardware.

Our vision

Artificial intelligence is changing the way we work. But not every organization wants to depend on a remote service to make use of its information.

ASYT explores a different approach: bringing AI capabilities closer to the user's documents, knowledge and infrastructure.

Our ambition is to build a local, adaptable and transparent AI environment in which users keep more control.

What guides us

Three core values.

Value 01

Privacy by design.

Favor local processing of information and limit unnecessary external exchanges.

Value 02

Technological independence.

Favor a modular architecture and the ability to choose compatible components.

Value 03

Continuous improvement.

Develop, test and evolve features while taking observed limitations into account.
