Features

Discover the possibilities of ASYT.

A local platform to chat with AI, explore your documents, retrieve knowledge and develop new workflows.

ASYT is software under development: feature availability and maturity may vary between versions.

01 / Conversation

Local conversational AI

Chat with compatible language models running on your own infrastructure.

  • Compatible language models, running on your own infrastructure.
  • Performance depends on the chosen model and the available hardware.
  • The initial download of models may require an Internet connection.
  • Local features are designed to work offline once the required components are installed.
02 / Documents

Documents and RAG search

Find the useful passages in your documents to enrich the AI's answers.

  • Import your documents, then query them in natural language.
  • Flow: documents, indexing, search, contextual answer.
  • Source references shown when the software version can provide them.
  • Supported formats and performance depend on the version.
03 / Memory

Local memory

Build continuity across conversations with persistent memory that is independent of the model. Feature under validation.

  • Local memory, independent of the language model.
  • Goal: keep and retrieve useful information across conversations.
  • Feature under validation: saving, retrieving and deleting information must be checked before any sensitive use.
  • Knowledge organization: the knowledge graph and 2D/3D visualization are capabilities in development.
04 / Automation

Intelligent automation

Explore scheduling and controlled execution of tasks with the HERMES module, in development.

  • Scheduling and execution of certain tasks with local tools and models.
  • Designed to run under the user's control, with appropriate controls and permissions.
  • Availability and permissions depend on the version and configuration.
  • Not every computing task can be automated.
05 / Models

Models and customization

Adapt the models and settings of your environment to the resources available.

  • Choose models compatible with your hardware.
  • Adjust settings to the resources available.
  • A compatible GPU can improve performance.
  • Not every model runs on every computer: configurations must be assessed case by case.
